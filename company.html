Company profile

Professional hot spring equipment solutions for commercial spa and wellness projects.

Beijing Yuanwang specializes in hot spring and spa equipment for international markets. We provide complete system solutions including water treatment, circulation, heating and control systems, designed for stable operation, energy efficiency and long-term use in commercial spa and wellness projects.

  • 2002Water engineering company founded
  • 2006Design institute founded
  • 100+Completed or ongoing projects
  • 50+Design professionals

Planning and design

Beijing Yuanwang BaiLi Landscape Architecture Design Co., Ltd., founded in 2006, works on hot spring towns, hotels, spas, wellness venues, water parks and premium pools. The company profile records over 100 completed or ongoing projects and a design team of more than 50 professionals.

Water engineering and installation

Beijing Yuanwang Liye Leisure Equipment Co., Ltd., founded in 2002, designs water treatment for geothermal water, hot springs and pools, and provides equipment, installation, commissioning and after-sales support.

Qualifications and patents

The August 2024 company profile lists Grade A environmental art design, Grade III mechanical and electrical installation, a safety production licence and utility models covering bathing pools, water treatment, hydrotherapy and pipe freeze protection.

What we do

Consulting, planning and design

Hot spring towns, hot spring hotels, water parks, spas, wellness resorts and premium pools; fountains, water features and irrigation; equipment selection for spas, water parks and swimming pools.

Equipment and MEP engineering

Water treatment for hot spring, geothermal and pool water; MEP installation, commissioning and maintenance for standard and custom pools, spas and hot spring venues; steam and sauna rooms; energy-saving and liquid-desiccant dehumidification design; investment cost analysis and technical guidance.

Core technologies

Hot spring and geothermal water treatment · premium spa equipment · family water play equipment · artificial hot springs, steam and sauna · geothermal cascade use · heat-driven liquid-desiccant dehumidification · light-and-sound immersive spas · smart hot spring and geothermal control systems.
