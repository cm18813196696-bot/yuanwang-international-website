Company profile

Planning and design

Beijing Yuanwang BaiLi Landscape Architecture Design Co., Ltd., founded in 2006, works on hot spring towns, hotels, spas, wellness venues, water parks and premium pools. The company profile records over 100 completed or ongoing projects and a design team of more than 50 professionals.

Water engineering and installation

Beijing Yuanwang Liye Leisure Equipment Co., Ltd., founded in 2002, designs water treatment for geothermal water, hot springs and pools, and provides equipment, installation, commissioning and after-sales support.

Qualifications and patents

The August 2024 company profile lists Grade A environmental art design, Grade III mechanical and electrical installation, a safety production licence and utility models covering bathing pools, water treatment, hydrotherapy and pipe freeze protection.
