INTERNATIONAL TRADE / 国际贸易

Export readiness & compliance.

出口合规必须按具体产品、用途、安装地点和型号核实，不能根据公司介绍直接认定取得认证。

Market-specific requirements / 目的国要求

United Kingdom / 英国

Check applicable product safety and electrical requirements; determine if CE or UKCA marking applies to each equipment category and location. Pool installation and water hygiene are separate considerations.

European Union / 欧盟

Determine which EU legislation applies to electrical equipment, machinery, pressure systems and controls. Confirm technical files, testing and conformity assessment for the exact product.

United States / 美国

Check federal, state and local pool, spa, electrical and plumbing requirements. NSF/ANSI/CAN 50 may apply to some recreational-water equipment; this is not a blanket requirement or approval.

Custom stainless-steel pools / 定制不锈钢泡池

Review material grades, welding, water chemistry, structural loads, electrical interfaces, water treatment, packing, transport and destination installation rules.

Procurement documents / 采购资料

Model and drawings; bill of materials; material and test reports; electrical specification; water-quality analysis; operating instructions; HS classification; Incoterms; origin and shipping documentation.

Important / 重要说明： No CE, UKCA, UL, NSF or other certification is claimed for any model without evidence. This page is preliminary screening guidance, not a legal certification opinion. 未经证据核实，不宣称任何型号已取得认证。
