Export readiness & compliance.

出口合规必须按具体产品、用途、安装地点和型号核实，不能根据公司介绍直接认定取得认证。

Market-specific requirements / 目的国要求

United Kingdom / 英国

Check applicable product safety and electrical requirements; determine if CE or UKCA marking applies to each equipment category and location. Pool installation and water hygiene are separate considerations.

European Union / 欧盟

Determine which EU legislation applies to electrical equipment, machinery, pressure systems and controls. Confirm technical files, testing and conformity assessment for the exact product.

United States / 美国

Check federal, state and local pool, spa, electrical and plumbing requirements. NSF/ANSI/CAN 50 may apply to some recreational-water equipment; this is not a blanket requirement or approval.

Custom stainless-steel pools / 定制不锈钢泡池

Review material grades, welding, water chemistry, structural loads, electrical interfaces, water treatment, packing, transport and destination installation rules.

Procurement documents / 采购资料

Model and drawings; bill of materials; material and test reports; electrical specification; water-quality analysis; operating instructions; HS classification; Incoterms; origin and shipping documentation.

Important / 重要说明： No CE, UKCA, UL, NSF or other certification is claimed for any model without evidence. This page is preliminary screening guidance, not a legal certification opinion. 未经证据核实，不宣称任何型号已取得认证。

Project compliance review by market

Great Britain

Separate equipment conformity from site approvals. For a supplied pump skid or control panel, classify the product before choosing the CE or UKCA route. Check machinery, electrical safety, EMC and pressure-equipment rules where relevant. For commercial spa operation, HSG282 addresses infection risk, commissioning, water monitoring and maintenance.

GOV.UK · GB marking

HSE · HSG282

European Union

Determine which EU legislation applies to the assembly and to supplied components. Record the technical documentation, conformity assessment route, declaration, instructions and economic-operator responsibilities. Check the machinery transition against the date of placing on the market.

EUR-Lex · Regulation (EU) 2023/1230

United States

Confirm adopted state and local pool, building, electrical and plumbing rules with the authority having jurisdiction. Check any NSF/ANSI/CAN 50 listing by equipment category and exact model. Use the CDC Model Aquatic Health Code as design and operational guidance, and verify local adoption separately.

CDC · MAHC

Site acceptance and handover

Agree the design basis before quotation. Include circulation and treatment design, suction safety, electrical protection and bonding, material certificates, weld inspection, leak and pressure tests as applicable, dosing interlocks, commissioning records, O&M manuals and operator training.
