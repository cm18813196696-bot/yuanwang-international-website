Water-Source Heat-Pump Operation

Electrical input enables a heat pump to transfer low-grade source heat to a higher useful temperature. Cooling: compressor → condenser, rejecting heat to groundwater/cooling water → expansion valve → evaporator, absorbing heat from building chilled water → compressor. Heating: the condenser heats building water; the expansion valve reduces refrigerant pressure; the evaporator extracts source-water heat; vapour returns to the compressor.

Operating-Cost Comparison - Assumptions

Historical brochure assumptions: boiler efficiency 85%; gas RMB 2.6/m³; electricity RMB 0.68/kWh; year-round demand. Winter 24 h/day, with heating 70% of load; summer/shoulder seasons 16 h/day. Equivalent full-load factors account for variable load. Heat-pump example: 0.25 kWh electricity per kWh heat, costing RMB 0.17/kWh heat. Source boiler estimate: 0.1143 Nm³ gas per kWh heat, about RMB 0.30/kWh heat. These are historical modelling assumptions, not current prices, a quotation or guaranteed savings.

Well 3 - Hot-Water Preheating Example
