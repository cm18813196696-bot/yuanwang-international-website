Why Reinject Tailwater

Source describes declining water levels with increasing extraction: examples of 3-5 m/year decline and pump settings deepened from 150 to 300 m. Direct discharge can create thermal/chemical impacts; examples use 35°C and 4-30 g/L mineralisation. Site-specific hydrogeological assessment is required.
