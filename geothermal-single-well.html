Historical Policy Context

Brochure cites Hebei 2019 data: 2,305 plain-area geothermal wells, approximately 77% without compliant reinjection provision, alongside a 23.5% reinjection figure. It describes provincial support for heat extraction without net water withdrawal, simplified filing, subsidies, demonstrations and local standards. These are historical source statements; current local requirements and eligibility require confirmation.

Closed and Open Single-Well Systems

Closed-loop example: double-U HDPE pipes, water or antifreeze, and circulation pump. Source shallow examples: 80-150 m depth and 10-25°C ground temperature. Winter: collect ground heat and upgrade with a heat pump; summer: reject building heat to the ground. Open-loop example: pump groundwater through a plate exchanger and reinject through the same or a nearby well. Source calls for full same-aquifer reinjection. Shallow U-tube and deep fracture-enhanced systems are different technologies.

Bathing-Water Supply - Source Claims

Brochure repeats historical well/reinjection policy context and proposes single-well heat exchange for bathing-water preparation, also claiming mineral enrichment of mains water. A sealed heat exchanger heats water without transferring minerals or establishing natural mineral-water status. SOURCE PAGE 42 / 78

Hydraulic Fracturing Layout

Original illustration depicts shale-gas horizontal-well hydraulic fracturing, used as a general process picture. Tankers deliver water; pumps inject water/sand/chemicals; the well turns horizontal; pressure opens fractures, sand props them open, gas reaches surface storage, returned water goes for treatment. It mentions about 200 tanker loads and >1 million US gallons (3,785.4 m³). Those quantities describe the gas-industry illustration, not a Yuanwang geothermal specification. Key:shale;gas well;groundwater level;storage tank;fracture;subsurface depth in feet.
