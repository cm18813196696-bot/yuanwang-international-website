Specify the project

Architecture & acrylic
Confirm support, deflection, sight lines and acrylic junctions for elevated and cantilevered pool concepts.
Light, media & water
Coordinate light colour, projection angles, reflected images and water circulation together at concept stage.
Room layout
The catalogue includes 5–10 m² themed studies. Allow space for the equipment, access routes and servicing when setting the layout.

Applications & options

Skyline & forest

Transparent acrylic pools, cantilevered concepts, secluded forest baths and courtyard pools shaped around the view.

Sculptural bathing

Boat, crescent, ring, droplet and cloud-inspired forms create a focal point for hospitality spaces.

Starlight collection

Underwater lighting, luminous rings and panoramic projection develop a coordinated night-time atmosphere.

Immersive nature

Forest, rainforest, cherry blossom and firefly themes combine sound, imagery and responsive lighting.

Family & discovery

Ocean animals, castles, woodland creatures, constellations and space scenes support playful interactive environments.

Storm & volcano

Programmed projection, atmospheric effects and coordinated water movement form dramatic themed sequences.

Minimal & future

Floating pool concepts, holographic imagery and digital environments can be tailored to compact private suites.

Concept development

The brochure includes compact 5–10 m² scene concepts. Final layouts depend on equipment space, circulation and the project brief.
