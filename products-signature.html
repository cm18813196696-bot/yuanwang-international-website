Specify the project

Architecture & acrylic
Confirm support, deflection, sight lines and acrylic junctions for elevated and cantilevered pool concepts.
Light, media & water
Coordinate light colour, projection angles, reflected images and water circulation together at concept stage.
Room layout
The catalogue includes 5–10 m² themed studies. Allow space for the equipment, access routes and servicing when setting the layout.
