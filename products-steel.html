Specify the project

Water chemistry & steel
Compare 304 with 316 or 316L using site water analysis, including chlorides, temperature and treatment chemicals. Confirm the grade before fabrication.
Welds & shell thickness
The brochure describes full argon-arc welding and passivation, with ≥2 mm as a starting recommendation for frequent commercial use. Final plate and reinforcement are sized for the project loads.
Mixed-material edges
Allow for movement at stone and steel junctions, ventilation behind timber and service access around glass and acrylic assemblies.
Filtration & handover
Coordinate drains, inlets, filtration, temperature control and maintenance access. Agree inspection, testing and the handover scope in the quotation.

Applications & options

Private bathing

Prefabricated welded shells for guest suites, courtyards, spa hotels and refurbishment projects.

Material and finish

304, 316 and 316L options; brushed, mirror and matte finishes. Select the grade against the actual water analysis and operating conditions.

Architectural combinations

Stone coping, timber surrounds, glass panels, microcement and acrylic viewing sections can be developed with coordinated junction details.

Shapes and water features

Round, rectangular and sculptural shells; seating, handrails, steps, lighting and water jets can be coordinated within the design.

Project specification

Confirm dimensions, usable volume, water chemistry, plate thickness, surface finish, access, structural loads and filtration before manufacture.

Delivery and care

Water testing, design, fabrication, installation, commissioning and maintenance support. Agree warranty terms and inspection schedules in the quotation.
