GEOTHERMAL ENGINEERING / 地热工程

From the well to the heat user

Concept sequence: source well → filtration and heat exchange → heat users by temperature level → heat pump where viable → approved reinjection or discharge route.

Resource assessment

Review well logs, test yield, source temperature and water chemistry. Compare seasonal loads for pools, domestic hot water and space heating before sizing plant.

Heat exchange and cascade use

Use a heat exchanger to separate source and building circuits where appropriate. Allocate higher-temperature heat first, then evaluate pool heating, low-temperature networks or heat pumps for the remaining heat.

Water treatment and reinjection

Review scaling, corrosion, suspended solids and water chemistry. Select filtration, cleaning and monitoring provisions, and assess reinjection capacity and water compatibility separately from bathing-water treatment.

Medium-deep and hot dry rock

The company profile includes geothermal well drilling and completion, reservoir stimulation and enhanced geothermal systems (EGS). Feasibility depends on the reservoir, drilling data, injection response and local approvals.

Controls and commissioning

Specify temperature, flow, pressure and energy metering. Agree alarm thresholds, interlocks and fallback sequences. Commission under representative loads and record the operating baseline.

Design and handover drawings

Typical deliverables include a heat and mass balance, process flow diagram, P&ID, plant layout, equipment schedule, electrical single-line diagram, control narrative, test records and as-built drawings as agreed.

Engineering for indoor pools and hot springs

Liquid-desiccant dehumidification

A circulating solution absorbs moisture from the incoming air. A regeneration stage removes that moisture and restores the solution concentration for reuse.

Stratified air distribution

Side-mounted outlets serve the occupied zone while high-level exhaust removes warm, humid air. Airflow and condensation control are assessed for each venue.

Pool and geothermal water treatment

System selection begins with source-water testing, anticipated bather load and local requirements. Design can cover circulation, filtration, disinfection, commissioning and maintenance planning.
