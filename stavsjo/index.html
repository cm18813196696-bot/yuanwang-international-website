      05 · Cascade use of spring water · 温泉水梯级利用

      One well,
      four temperatures.

      Spring water gives its heat in steps — hottest uses first, then cooler ones, and finally water-source heat pumps — before it is reinjected at 20 °C or below.

      How a water-source heat pump works · 水源热泵原理
      It lifts low-grade heat from shallow groundwater or spent spring water to useful temperatures with a small input of electricity. In heating mode the refrigerant releases heat to the building's heating water in the condenser and picks up heat from the source water in the evaporator; in cooling mode the cycle is reversed and the heat goes into the source water.
      06 · Geothermal-driven liquid-desiccant dehumidification · 热驱动溶液除湿热泵

      Dry air,
      driven by spring water.

      Pool halls are humid. A liquid-desiccant heat pump removes moisture and odours without over-cooling and reheating the air, and its solution is regenerated with geothermal hot water — no other energy needed.

            Built once.

            How a STAVSJÖ pool is made — and why a welded steel shell outlasts concrete and tile.

            0Separate waterproof layers that can fail
            304 / 316LFood-grade stainless steel
            30+ yearsExpected life of the corrosion-resistant structure
            50%+Shorter build time than conventional construction
            The principle

            The shell is the waterproofing.

            Concrete pools rely on a coating or membrane to hold water. Over time it ages and cracks, and a leak usually means breaking out tiles and closing the pool. A STAVSJÖ pool is one fully welded stainless steel vessel: between the water and the structure there is only metal. The non-porous surface does not hold dirt or colour, which cuts chemical use and cleaning time.

            Fabrication

            Six steps in our factory.

            01

            Water first

            We test before we design. Chlorides, temperature and treatment chemicals decide the steel grade. For aggressive spring water we offer a steel structure with a dedicated liner.

            02

            Precision cutting

            Plates are cut by CNC laser or plasma for accurate, clean edges, which are then chamfered.

            03

            Forming

            Hydraulic press brakes and rollers shape each panel, with bend radii of at least 1.5 × plate thickness to avoid stress cracks.

            04

            TIG welding

            Full argon-arc welding with matched ER308L / ER316L filler, worked in staged sequences to control distortion. Reinforcing ribs are spaced at no more than 800 mm.

            05

            Finishing

            Welds are ground and the surface polished in stages (80 / 120 / 240 grit) to a brushed, mirror or matte finish, then passivated.

            06

            Inspection

            Material certificates checked; welds inspected visually and by dye-penetrant test, with radiographic or ultrasonic testing on critical joints; dimensions verified before dispatch.

            Best value

            SUS 304

            The standard choice for tap water and typical hot spring water — the balanced starting point for most projects.

            Coastal · sulphur

            SUS 316 / 316L

            Stronger resistance to chlorides and sulphides, for seaside sites, high-chlorine water, sulphur springs and heavy commercial use. About 20–30% higher material cost than 304.

            Why are you cheaper than European brands?

            We design, cut, weld and polish in our own factory and sell to you directly. There is no importer, distributor or showroom in between, so you pay the factory price for the same stainless steel grades.

            How do I get a price?

            Send us a size, a sketch or a photo of your site through the quote form, WhatsApp or email. We reply within 1 working day. Drawings and photos help us price faster and more accurately.

            Do you sell to my country?

            Yes. We work with clients in any country. Our group has projects in China and Sweden and has delivered or is delivering projects in Canada, New Zealand and the USA.

            Can I order a single unit?

            Yes. Every product is made to order, so one hot tub for a private villa is as welcome as a full hotel spa.

            Products & materials

            304 or 316L stainless steel — which do I need?

            SUS 304 suits most fresh-water pools and hot tubs. For mineral hot spring water, salt water or coastal sites we usually recommend 316L. Send us your source-water report and we will advise. Every shell uses steel with nickel content of at least 8%.

            Can you build to my exact size and shape?

            Yes. Size, shape, depth, finish, jets and lighting are all made to your specification. Try shapes and finishes in our 3D Design Studio, then send us a screenshot.

            How do you check quality?

            Welded shells are TIG-welded, dye-penetrant tested and given a 24-hour water-tightness test. See Quality & Supply Chain for the full list of checks.

            Are the pictures on this site real?

            Most photographs come from our factory and our projects. Some images are design renderings, and they are labelled. Final specifications always follow the approved drawings.

            Can I visit the factory?

            Yes, by appointment. We are happy to show customers round the production campus in Beijing, or give you a live video tour.

            Shipping & installation

            How long does production take?

            It depends on the size, finish and equipment. Your quotation states the production time and delivery schedule before you order.

            How is it shipped?

            Products are packed for export in our factory. The delivery terms — to your nearest port or to your site — are agreed and written into your quotation.

            Who installs it?

            You choose one of three modes. A · Guided: our engineer attends and your team installs. B · Turnkey: our team installs and commissions. C · Self-install: drawings, video guidance and remote commissioning. Read the details.

            Will the equipment work with my local power supply?

            Tell us your country and the power supply on site. Pumps, heaters and controls are specified for it and confirmed in the quotation.

            Warranty & service

            What warranty do I get?

            Stainless steel shell: 5 years. Buried pipework: 3 years. Equipment: 2 years. Wellness equipment: 1 year. Wear parts: 3 months. The warranty starts when commissioning is signed off.

            What if something breaks far from China?

            We solve most issues remotely, by video call and remote access to the control system. Standard spare parts ship within 15 working days, custom parts within 45, and key parts stay available for 8 years. On-site visits are arranged as your contract states.

            Which language do you work in?

            English and Chinese. Manuals are supplied in English.
