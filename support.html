LIFECYCLE SERVICES

Support beyond installation.

Commissioning, operator handover, planned maintenance, troubleshooting, spare parts coordination and system optimisation — tailored to the project contract.

01 / SUPPORT SCOPE

Keep complex systems working.

01 / START-UP

Commissioning & acceptance

System checks, test records, operator sign-off and handover documentation within contracted scope.

02 / TRAINING

Operator training

Water-quality routines, pump and filter operation, safety checks, control panel use and escalation procedures.

03 / MAINTENANCE

Preventive maintenance

Scheduled inspections of pumps, filters, valves, dosing, heat exchangers, sensors and control cabinets.

04 / RESPONSE

Fault diagnosis & repair coordination

Troubleshooting using logs, alarm histories and site feedback; remote or on-site assistance subject to agreement.

05 / PARTS

Spare parts & consumables

Plan replacement filters, seals, sensors, valves and other approved spare parts around equipment lifecycle.

06 / OPTIMISATION

Energy & water optimisation

Review circulation, heating, heat recovery, control setpoints and operating data to identify improvement opportunities.

02 / SERVICE REQUEST

Get the right engineer involved.

When requesting support, include project name, installed equipment model, serial number if available, alarm screenshots, water-quality readings, date of occurrence and photos or video.

Email technical support request

No response-time guarantee, international on-site coverage or warranty duration is stated without a signed service agreement.
